## Official policy and licence findings

- [Pixabay licence](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) and
  [full terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/): commercial use and adaptation
  are permitted subject to restrictions, including standalone redistribution
  and third-party rights. Source listings do not independently establish ownership.
- [Freesound FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/) and
  [CC0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): CC0 recordings
  permit commercial reuse. Automated copyright claims can still occur; retain
  source dates and licence records for disputes. The selected audio metadata
  records CC0 for 695619, 651189 and 788146.
- [YouTube monetisation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en):
  permission alone does not resolve reused-content eligibility. Repetitive,
  interchangeable or mass-produced videos with minimal variation risk losing
  monetisation. Combining different stock IDs is not proof of substantive
  originality. This is the principal risk assessed for the present loop format.
- [YouTube AI disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en):
  clearly non-realistic animation generally does not require disclosure under
  the realistic synthetic-content rule; other policies still apply.
- [Freesound API terms](https://freesound.org/help/tos_api/) and
  [official commercial-use explanation](https://blog.freesound.org/?p=2051):
  commercial use of the Application Programming Interface (API) needs a separate
  commercial agreement. The sound's CC0 licence does not grant that API access.
  Confirm applicable access terms before enabling commercial automated discovery.
- [Artlist licence](https://artlist.io/help-center/privacy-terms/artlist-license):
  standalone use is restricted, with further restrictions on music/sound-effect
  reproduction and looping music videos. Do not assume a standard subscription
  covers an ambient rain channel; obtain confirmation for this exact format first.

## Recommended supply strategy

1. Use Pixabay's matching contributor portfolios and exact reference scenes for
   near-term video discovery; retain source/licence evidence at import. No claim
   is made that enough fresh matching stock exists for indefinite daily uploads.
2. Use Freesound CC0 rain recordings for near-term audio candidates, with searches
   for indoor rain, window rain and soft steady rain. Listen before inclusion:
   tags cannot establish absence of speech, music, traffic or thunder. Keep the
   three accepted recordings available while replacements are evaluated.
3. For a sustainable distinctive library, record or commission rain audio and
   commission original anime scenes/animation. Obtain written rights for editing,
   repeated YouTube publication and monetisation; clarify any exclusivity.
4. Keep the style consistent while each release has materially different scene
   composition and sound. Cosmetic edits, speed/pitch changes, new thumbnails and
   permutations of the same assets do not establish originality or policy safety.
